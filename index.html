Welcome to Rebooting Motherhood

Hi! I'm Ashley, the mom behind Rebooting Motherhood. I'm a mom of two amazing kids, have a full-time career in IT, am a caregiver, a coffee enthusiast, a fitness hopeful, and someone who firmly believes that sometimes laughter is the only reasonable response to the absolute chaos of life.

My life isn't exactly what anyone would call simple. I'm a single mom juggling motherhood, navigating the unique challenges of raising children with additional needs, managing a career and a household, trying to take care of myself, and occasionally attempting to remember what day it is. And honestly? Life can get pretty overwhelming.

That's why I created Rebooting Motherhood.

Rebooting Motherhood is about finding yourself again.

Not by pretending life is perfect. Not by waking up at 4 a.m. to drink a green smoothie and become a completely different person by Tuesday.

But by making small changes, finding support, laughing when things go sideways, taking care of our bodies and minds, and giving ourselves permission to start over as many times as necessary.

Whether you're navigating motherhood, raising children with additional needs, trying to improve your health, balancing a career, rebuilding your confidence, or simply trying to survive the week without losing your damn mind...

You belong here.

You don't have to have it all figured out. You don't have to do it alone. And you absolutely do not need to wait until Monday to start over.

Sometimes a reboot starts with a cup of coffee. Sometimes it starts with a workout. Sometimes it starts with asking for help.

And sometimes it starts with saying, "Well, that was a shitshow. Let's try again."

You don't need a perfect life to make progress. You just need a place to start.
